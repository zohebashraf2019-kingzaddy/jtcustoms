Can you do a starlight on a car with a sunroof?

Yes — we build around sunroofs and panoramic roofs, and can outline the opening with stars. Toggle “Sunroof” in the Starlight Studio to preview it.

How many stars should I get?

Kits run from 300 to 1000+ stars. Smaller cabins look great at 300–450; larger SUVs, trucks and vans fill out best with 600–1000. Try a few counts in the studio and see what you like.

Can I get a custom design or shooting stars?

Yes — shooting stars, two-tone kits and custom designs like names and logos are all available as add-ons.

How do I book?

DM @jtcustomautos on Instagram with your vehicle. A deposit is required for all bookings.
